    Ranks and rules
    • Pieces move one square up, down or sideways. Scouts move any distance in a straight line.
    • The higher rank wins an attack. Equal ranks destroy each other.
    • Miners defuse Bombs. The Spy defeats the Marshal only when the Spy attacks.
