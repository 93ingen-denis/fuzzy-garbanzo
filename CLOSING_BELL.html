US markets

Wall Street's AI sell-off deepens: S&P 500 falls 3.7% into a correction

A seventh straight daily loss left the S&P 500 down 8.6% on the week. Futures point to a partial rebound on Monday.

Wall Street · Friday 20 November 2026 close, with Monday 23 November early trading

US stocks plunged on Friday as the unwinding of the artificial-intelligence trade spread across markets. The S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910.39, entering correction territory. The Nasdaq composite lost 5.3% to 22,617.73 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.9% to 48,147.33.

  • Weekly scorecard: the S&P 500 fell 8.6%, the Dow 8% and the Nasdaq 10%.
  • Nvidia: shares dropped 14% in the sell-off. The VIX volatility index jumped 26% to 35.89.
  • Rates: Treasuries rallied as investors sought safety. The 10-year yield fell more than 10 basis points to 4.94% and the 2-year to below 4.65%. Markets still price about a 98% chance of a Federal Reserve rate hike on 9 December.
  • Other markets: gold fell 4% on Friday and silver 7.3%. Bitcoin briefly dropped near $57,800.

On Monday morning S&P 500 futures were up about 2.2% and Nasdaq 100 futures up 2.8%, a partial rebound. Gold was up 2.4% at about $3,625 and the dollar gave back part of Friday's gains.

Japan

Nikkei plunges 5% as the AI rout hits Tokyo; markets closed Monday for a holiday

Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 5.06% to 56,436.65 on Friday, led by SoftBank and Advantest, a weekly loss of about 7.7% from the previous Friday's 61,142.35. Other Asian markets fell sharply too: Taiwan's Taiex lost 6.23% and South Korea's Kospi 5.21%.

The Tokyo Stock Exchange is closed today, Monday 23 November, for a public holiday, so the next Nikkei close is Tuesday. Futures were trading below Friday's close ahead of the holiday. The index is now about 22.5% below its record close of 72,831.73 from 22 June.

Euro zone

European stocks fall for a fifth day as tech sell-off hits ASML

The pan-European STOXX 600 fell 2.00% to 625.65 on Friday, its fifth straight loss and about 5.8% lower on the week. The blue-chip Euro STOXX 50 dropped 2.81% to 5,941.95, a weekly loss of about 7.0% from 6,386.86 a week earlier.

All eight STOXX 600 sectors fell. Technology lost 5.39%, basic resources 3.54% and banks 3.05%, while health care held up best at −0.16%. ASML dropped 9.3%. Early on Monday, European stocks were edging higher as the FTSE 100 and DAX rebounded.

France

CAC 40 falls 1.9% to its lowest close in weeks as tech selling spreads

Paris stocks closed sharply lower on Friday. The CAC 40 lost 1.89% to 7,927.75, ending the week 5.27% below the previous Friday's close of 8,368.91, which had been a one-month high. Every sector of the STOXX 600 fell, with banks and resources hit hardest.

The index is now about 9.2% below its record close of 8,726.03 set on 10 August. The Paris market opens today alongside a partial rebound in US futures.
