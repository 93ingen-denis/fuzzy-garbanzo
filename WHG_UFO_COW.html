🛸 Night Harvest

Fly your saucer over the farm and abduct 8 cows. Switch on the tractor beam over a cow and keep her perfectly still for 5 seconds – hover slowly, fast flying spooks the herd. Then the lift begins (and she will moo).

⚠ Crows dive at your saucer – they can’t damage it, but a hit jams the beam for ~2 seconds: the scan resets and a cow being lifted drops. 🐂 Bulls are hostile and ram you when you fly low. Higher = safer from bulls but wider beam is easier to spot by crows.

Move WASD / arrows / joystick · Beam hold Space / BEAM · Altitude Q·E / ▲▼ / wheel · Laser F / 🔫 – auto-aims at the nearest crow and shoots it down
