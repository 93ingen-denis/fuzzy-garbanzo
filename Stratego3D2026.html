Stratego: Napoleonic 3D

Victory!
How to play

You are red (bottom). Pieces are sculpted Napoleonic-style miniatures: foot soldiers for the low ranks, plumed officers, mounted colonel, general and marshal, a standard with an eagle for the Flag and a cannon for the Bomb. Ranks are written on the bases. Use the 2D button for a top-down view and 3D to go back. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Setup: click one of your pieces, then another, to swap them (or shuffle). Press Start battle. Click a piece, then a highlighted square. Blue's pieces stay hidden until they fight.

Higher rank wins; equal ranks both die. Spy (1) beats the Marshal (10) only when the spy attacks. Miners (3) defuse Bombs; any other piece that hits a Bomb dies. Scouts (2) move any distance in a straight line. Bombs and the Flag cannot move. Capture the enemy Flag, or leave them with no moves, to win. Lakes block movement.
